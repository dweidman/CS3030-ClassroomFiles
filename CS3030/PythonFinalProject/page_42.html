    Credits: (1)

    Typically Taught Fall Semester: 1st Blk

    Course Fee Purpose: This course has a fee attached.     Description: The Leave No Trace (LNT) Trainer course is designed to train current and future outdoor recreation leaders on the principles of Leave No Trace.
